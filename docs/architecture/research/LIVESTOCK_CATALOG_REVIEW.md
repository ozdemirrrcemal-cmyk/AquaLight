# Livestock catalog review (internal, not packaged in the app)

This is a review ledger for `app/src/main/assets/livestock_catalog.jsonl`. It is not a source field in the product catalog or a user-facing attribution screen. Facts and independently chosen care ranges may be used; do not copy source prose, images, or an entire third-party compilation into the app.

## Review rules

- Match the scientific taxon and record type before comparing care values. A genus group, trade form, wild population, breeding setup, and captive aquarium animal need not share one range.
- Check each relevant field separately. A temperature recommendation does not verify nitrate, and a bare `ppm` value does not establish its analyte or reporting basis.
- Prefer species-specific aquarium husbandry information for a care range; use field observations and broad group sheets as context, not as interchangeable thresholds.
- Keep the shipped catalog free of source URLs, review flags and copied passages. Record investigation and disagreements here. Do not label a row fully reviewed while material fields remain unresolved.
- A SOFT care range is guidance, not a toxicity or disease threshold.
