Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This policy explains which data AquaLight processes, where it is stored, why it is used, and the choices and rights available to you. It reflects the current Android application.

1. Controller and contact

AquaLight is operated by My AquaLight in Türkiye. Privacy questions, requests, and complaints may be sent to support@myaqualight.com.

2. Data processed by AquaLight

Account and authentication data

  • Email address, Firebase user identifier, authentication provider, and account status.
  • When Google sign-in is used, the account name, email address, and profile image made available through that sign-in flow.
  • Technical security information processed by Firebase Authentication, such as IP address and user-agent data, for authentication and abuse prevention.

Data stored locally on your Android device

  • Profile details you enter, including name, username, address, phone number, country, and profile image.
  • Aquarium and tank details, notes, measurements, schedules, livestock, plants, materials, and tank images.
  • Registered-device records, tank assignments, provisioning state, device credentials, reminder settings, and local notification data.
  • Local usage counters shown inside the application.

These product records remain in app-controlled local storage and are not uploaded to an analytics service.

Feedback and support data

When you send feedback through the app, AquaLight processes the category, message, optional reply email address, app version, selected application language, and your Firebase user identifier when signed in. Eligible signed-out submissions use an anonymous marker.

Permissions and device access

  • Camera access is used for QR scanning and selected local profile or aquarium image flows.
  • Photo access is used only when you choose an image for a local product feature.
  • Bluetooth, nearby-device, Wi-Fi, and location-related permissions are used where Android requires them for device discovery, provisioning, or connected-network information.
  • AquaLight does not use these permissions for continuous location tracking.

3. Telemetry not collected

The current commercial configuration does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Firebase Performance Monitoring. AquaLight does not use those SDKs to collect app-usage, crash-reporting, or performance-monitoring telemetry.

4. Purposes and legal grounds

  • Creating and securing accounts and maintaining authenticated sessions.
  • Providing aquarium management, connected-device setup, reminders, and local app features.
  • Receiving, investigating, and replying to feedback and support requests.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and establishing or defending legal claims.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may rely on performance of the service relationship, legal obligations, legitimate interests that do not override your rights, or consent where consent is legally required.

5. Service providers and international processing

AquaLight uses Firebase Authentication for account access and Cloud Firestore for text feedback. The production Firestore database is configured in europe-west1. Firebase Authentication may involve processing in the United States. Google generally processes customer data under the applicable Firebase and Google Cloud terms.

6. Sharing

Personal data is not sold. It may be shared only with service providers needed to operate the functions described here, when required by binding law or a competent authority, to protect users and the service against abuse or security threats, or as part of a lawful business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while the account remains active and as required for security or legal obligations. Local aquarium and device data remains on the Android device until you delete it, delete the account through the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app, subject to Android storage behavior.

Text feedback records are retained only for support, security, and legal needs and are removed under AquaLight’s documented retention and deletion process when no longer necessary.

The in-app account-deletion flow is designed to remove the Firebase account, owner-linked feedback records, and account-scoped local data. Contact support if the flow cannot be completed.

8. Security

AquaLight uses account-scoped access controls, Firebase security rules, app-private or encrypted local storage where appropriate, HTTPS transport, and least-privilege data access. No technical system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about processing, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or data portability, and may complain to the competent data-protection authority. You may withdraw consent for processing that relies on consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send requests to support@myaqualight.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

10. Age eligibility

AquaLight is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for or collect personal data from persons under 18.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated when the product, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change. The revision date will be updated and material changes will be communicated where required.
