2. Product privacy summary

  • AquaLight uses Firebase only for Authentication and text feedback stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Realtime Database, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging, advertising SDKs, or behavioural tracking.
  • Tank records, profile details, photos, maintenance records, reminders, connected-device records, and on-device usage counters remain in the app's private local storage and are not uploaded by AquaLight.
  • Location coordinates are not collected. On older Android versions, location permission may be required by Android to read the current Wi-Fi network or scan for Bluetooth devices.
  • The feedback form does not capture or upload screenshots.

4. Service providers, regions, and transfers

Google LLC provides Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and optional Google Sign-In. Google acts under the applicable Firebase/Google terms and data-processing terms. Google's published Firebase subprocessor information forms part of that provider framework.

  • Cloud Firestore: feedback is stored in the regional europe-west1 location (Belgium).
  • Firebase Authentication: Google states that this service is operated from United States data centres.
  • Support and security operations: authorised provider personnel or subprocessors may process data where Google maintains facilities, subject to the applicable terms and safeguards.

Where personal data is transferred outside Türkiye, the EEA, or your country, AquaLight applies the transfer mechanism required by applicable law, including the relevant KVKK Article 9 safeguard and/or EU Standard Contractual Clauses where required. No personal data is sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Security

AquaLight uses transport encryption, Firebase security rules tied to the authenticated UID, encrypted local stores for sensitive device/provisioning data, private app storage, no Android cloud backup, memory-only Firestore caching, bounded input, and account reauthentication before deletion. No system can be guaranteed absolutely secure; please protect your device and account credentials and report suspected incidents promptly.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, including Türkiye's Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the GDPR, you may request information and access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, data portability, and information about recipients or transfers. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. AquaLight currently has no optional analytics or advertising consent.

Send requests through the privacy contact channel in section 1. We will respond within the period required by the law applicable to your request.
